# Samfunnsfag (SAF01-05)

Checkpoints after 2., 4., 7. and 10. trinn. Samfunnsfag is where the line
between *knows* and *has an opinion* matters most. Pensum grades the first and
never the second. A pupil can be asked what a source says, what happened, what
a right protects, or which reason a named person gives. A pupil is never asked
which party, belief or opinion is right.

**Watch for:** *old* treated as one period (Vikings and grandparents in the
same "long ago"); a map read with north as "up the hill"; *democracy* reduced to
voting; a source judged by whether the pupil agrees with it.

## Activities

- **Then and now** (`sort`, P, 2.–4.). Photos of objects: *grandparents' time*,
  *now*, *both*. Then a timeline `sequence` of four objects. The fixed-scale
  timeline is always drawn with the pupil's own birth year marked, so "long
  ago" has an anchor.
- **Map detective** (`label`, P). Place names and symbols on a map; later,
  tap the place that fits a description (*a fjord with a town at the end*).
- **Fact, opinion, advert** (`sort`, P, 4.–7.). Shared with norsk.
- **Two newspapers** (`highlight`, P, 7.–10.). Two made-up articles on one
  event; tap what differs, then pick why they might differ.
- **Whose reason** (`perspective`, P). *The council wants a road through the
  park. Which reason would Siri, who walks her dog there, give?* Graded on the
  text; the pupil's own view is not asked for.
- **Class vote** (`dialogue` + mission, 2.–4.). A scripted class meeting where
  the pupil proposes, listens and votes; followed by a real class vote.
- **Rights match** (`match`, P). Situation to the right that protects it.
- **Budget bars** (`bar_model`, P). Pocket money as a bar; drag in spending.
  Shared with matematikk strand 11.
- **Share or not** (`sort`, P). *Safe to share*, *ask an adult first*, *never
  share*: name, school, a photo of your house, favourite colour.
- **Cause and consequence** (`cause_effect`, P, 7.–10.). Industrialisation to
  urbanisation; drought to migration.

## Sensitive content

Violence, abuse and boundaries (KM14676, KM14727, KM14694, KM14711), and
genocide and terrorism (KM14705), are in the progression guide because the
curriculum puts them there. On screen:

- factual items only, no rewards, no map growth, not shown on the class grid;
- every screen touching abuse or boundaries shows, in the same place each time,
  the national helpline for children and young people (Alarmtelefonen for barn
  og unge, 116 111) and "talk to an adult you trust";
- no scenario asks the pupil about their own experience.
